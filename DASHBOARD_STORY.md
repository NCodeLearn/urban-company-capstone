# Urban Company Service-Ops Dashboard Story

## 1. City Ops Lead

**Headline:** Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate among the six cities.

**Evidence:** Bengaluru recorded 17 SLA breaches across 107 bookings, giving an SLA breach rate of 15.89%. Across the full reconciled dataset, there are 79 SLA breaches across 600 bookings, which is an overall SLA breach rate of 13.2%.

**Implication:** The City Ops Lead should first review the operating reasons behind Bengaluru's higher breach rate and identify whether particular service categories are contributing more strongly to missed SLAs.

## 2. Category Lead

**Headline:** Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest revenue category, while Salon for Women is comparatively under-booked.

**Evidence:** Deep Home Cleaning generated INR 508,964 from 176 bookings. In comparison, Salon for Women recorded 65 bookings and generated INR 151,689. These figures are taken directly from the reconciled dashboard data.

**Implication:** The Category Lead can use the stronger category as a benchmark for demand and revenue performance, while reviewing the comparatively lower booking volume in Salon for Women to understand whether the difference is caused by demand, partner availability, pricing, or city coverage.
